# Rohan's Creative Space

Create a clean, modern, professional personal portfolio website for Rohan Sharma, a B.Com student currently studying in the 2nd year, 3rd semester at Surya Sen Mahavidyalaya.

1. Hero / Home Section

Create a visually strong but minimal hero section featuring:

* Name: Rohan Sharma
* Short headline: B.Com Student | Creative Mind | Gamer | Painter | Cooking Enthusiast
* Brief introduction explaining that Rohan is currently pursuing his B.Com degree and has interests in gaming, painting, and cooking.
* Include a professional profile picture/avatar area prominently in the hero section. Use a clean circular or subtly rounded image frame with a modern border/glow effect.
* Add clear CTA buttons such as “About Me” and “Get in Touch”.
* Use subtle animations when the page loads, keeping them smooth and professional.

2. About Me

Create an About section that presents Rohan in a friendly and professional way.

Content:

* Rohan Sharma is currently pursuing a Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com).
* He is in his 2nd year, 3rd semester at Surya Sen Mahavidyalaya.
* He is interested in creative and recreational activities including:
    * 🎮 Gaming
    * 🎨 Painting
    * 🍳 Cooking
* Mention that he is currently focused on developing himself academically and personally while exploring his interests and future opportunities.

Use a modern two-column layout with the profile image on one side and the biography on the other.

3. Education

Create a dedicated Education section with a clean timeline/card design.

Include:
Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com)

* Institution: Surya Sen Mahavidyalaya
* Current status: 2nd Year, 3rd Semester
* Graduation year: Not specified — do not invent one

Clearly mark the education as Currently Pursuing.

4. Skills & Interests

Create a visually engaging section showcasing Rohan’s current skills and areas of interest:

* Gaming
* Painting
* Cooking

Since these are interests rather than formally certified professional skills, present them honestly as Skills & Interests rather than claiming professional expertise.

Use elegant cards with simple icons and subtle hover animations.

5. Experience

Create a simple section explaining that Rohan is currently building his experience.

Content:
No professional work experience yet

* Currently focused on education, personal development, and exploring creative interests.
* Design this positively without making the section feel empty or unfinished.

6. Services

Because Rohan currently does not offer professional services, do not fabricate services.

Instead, create a small section titled “What I’m Exploring” or “Currently Exploring”, explaining that he is developing his skills and discovering areas where he can build professional expertise in the future.

7. Projects

Create a Projects / Coming Soon section.

Content:
Projects Coming Soon
“I’m currently learning, exploring, and working toward building projects that showcase my skills and creativity.”

Make this section visually interesting rather than simply leaving it blank. Use a subtle “Coming Soon” card or futuristic placeholder design.

8. Contact Section

Create a clean contact section with:

* Email: rohanshamra33@gmail.com
* Phone: 8250985542
* Instagram username: @lill.stirrr07

Add modern contact cards/icons and clickable actions where appropriate.

Include a simple contact form with:

* Name
* Email
* Message
* Send Message button

The form should have a polished UI, but do not claim that messages are actually sent unless a backend/email service is implemented.

9. Navigation

Create a responsive navigation bar containing:

* Home
* About
* Education
* Skills
* Experience
* Projects
* Contact

On mobile, convert it into a clean hamburger menu.

The navbar should remain accessible while scrolling and have a subtle backdrop/blur effect.

10. Visual Design

The overall design must feel:

* Clean
* Modern
* Minimal
* Professional
* Young and creative
* Premium without being overly flashy

Use plenty of whitespace, strong typography, rounded cards, subtle shadows, smooth transitions, and a carefully balanced layout.

Use a modern neutral color palette, preferably a clean white/off-white background with charcoal/black typography and one tasteful accent color. Avoid excessive gradients, neon colors, or clutter.

Typography should be modern and highly readable, with a strong bold display font for headings and a clean sans-serif font for body text.

11. Animations & Interactions

Add subtle modern interactions:

* Smooth scrolling
* Fade/slide-in animations on sections
* Hover effects on cards and buttons
* Animated navigation state
* Gentle profile-image hover effect
* Scroll reveal animations
* Button micro-interactions

Keep animations fast, smooth, and professional. Do not overuse animations.

12. Responsive Design

The website must work perfectly on:

* Mobile phones
* Tablets
* Laptops
* Desktop monitors

Pay particular attention to mobile typography, navigation, spacing, profile image sizing, and contact information.

13. Footer

Create a minimal footer containing:

* Rohan Sharma
* Short tagline such as “Learning. Creating. Growing.”
* Instagram link
* Email link
* Copyright notice

14. Important Accuracy Rules

Do not invent:

* Work experience
* Companies
* Internships
* Certifications
* Professional services
* Projects
* Graduation year
* Professional skills that Rohan has not provided

Clearly represent him as a current B.Com student with interests in gaming, painting, and cooking.

The final website should feel like a polished student portfolio, not an exaggerated corporate résumé.

Overall Goal

Build a visually impressive portfolio that introduces Rohan Sharma as a young B.Com student who is currently learning, developing himself, and exploring his creative interests. The design should communicate confidence, simplicity, creativity, and potential, while remaining truthful to his current experience level.

Make the final result feel like a portfolio that Rohan could continue expanding later as he gains skills, completes projects, and builds professional experience.
